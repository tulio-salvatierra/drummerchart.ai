The pitch

I've spent more Saturday nights charting a setlist than actually playing one — highlighting a lyric sheet, scrubbing back ten seconds at a time, guessing whether that's a pre-chorus or just a long verse.

This is the tool I wanted before every fill-in gig I ever took. Upload the track, get the map, show up ready.

How it
works

    Upload the track
    An MP3 is all it takes to start.

  3. 3
